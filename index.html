Party Games

🐘 White elephant

One wrapped item from your house, one pile, and a little friendly stealing. Here are the rules, in writing, so there's nothing to argue about on the night.

  1. 1

    Bring one wrapped item from your house that you want gone. Funny regift energy. Nobody shops for this, and there's no price talk.

  2. 2

    No names on the gifts. All the gifts go in one pile.

  3. 3

    Everyone draws a number. Number 1 picks a gift from the pile and opens it.

  4. 4

    In number order, each person either opens a new gift from the pile or steals one that's already open. If yours gets stolen, you right away open a new one or steal from someone else — but you can't steal back the gift that was just taken from you.

  5. 5

    A gift can only be stolen twice in the whole game. After that, it's frozen with whoever's holding it.

  6. 6

    After the last person goes, Number 1 gets one final swap with any opened gift. That's the end — no extra exchange round.

  7. 7

    After the game, trades are fine if both people agree and announce it out loud.
